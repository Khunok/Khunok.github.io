  • အင်တာနက်မရှိချိန် ပြနိုင်ရန် မိုးလေဝသ၊ ငွေလဲနှုန်း၊ ရွှေဈေး ယာယီမှတ်ထားချက် (cache)

အင်တာနက်မှ ရယူသော ဝန်ဆောင်မှုများ

Summary

  • No account is needed. We do not ask for your name, phone number or email.
  • Your appointments, document expiry dates and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us.
  • The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking. We do not sell or share personal data.
  • The app does not use GPS location. You choose your city yourself.

Data stored on your device

  • Settings such as language, text size and colour theme
  • Appointments you add (doctor, meeting, trip, ceremony) and 90-day report, visa, work permit and passport dates
  • Your chosen city, recent city searches and your own exchange rate
  • Cached weather, exchange rates and gold prices so the app works offline

You can delete items inside the app. Uninstalling the app, or Settings → Apps → Clear data, removes all of it.

Online services

Some information is fetched directly from the third-party services below. As with any internet request, these services can see your IP address. For weather, only the coordinates of the city you selected, or the city name you search for, are sent. No personal information is sent.

Permissions

  • Internet – to fetch the information above
  • Notifications – reminders for appointments and expiry dates, scheduled on your device (not sent from a server)
  • Phone dialer – tapping an emergency number only opens your phone's dialer; the app does not read call logs

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal information from anyone.
